===============================================================================
                       C OPERATOR PRECEDENCE TABLE
===============================================================================
 Precedence: 1 (Highest) -> 15 (Lowest)
===============================================================================

Level  Operators                  Description                    Associativity
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  1    ()                         Function call                  Left-to-right
       []                         Array subscripting
       .                          Structure/union member
       ->                         Structure/union pointer member
       (type){list}               Compound literal (C99)

  2    ++ --                      Postfix increment / decrement  Right-to-left
       + -                        Unary plus / minus
       ! ~                        Logical NOT / Bitwise NOT
       (type)                     Type cast
       *                          Indirection (dereference)
       &                          Address-of
       sizeof                     Size-of operator
       _Alignof                   Alignment requirement (C11)

  3    * / %                      Multiplication / Division /    Left-to-right
                                  Remainder (Modulus)

  4    + -                        Addition / Subtraction         Left-to-right

  5    << >>                      Bitwise left shift / right     Left-to-right
                                  shift

  6    < <=                       Relational less than /         Left-to-right
       > >=                       less than or equal to,
                                  greater than /
                                  greater than or equal to

  7    == !=                      Equality / Inequality          Left-to-right

  8    &                          Bitwise AND                    Left-to-right

  9    ^                          Bitwise XOR (exclusive OR)     Left-to-right

 10    |                          Bitwise OR (inclusive OR)      Left-to-right

 11    &&                         Logical AND                    Left-to-right

 12    ||                         Logical OR                     Left-to-right

 13    ?:                         Ternary conditional            Right-to-left

 14    =                          Simple assignment              Right-to-left
       += -=                      Compound assignment
       *= /= %=
       <<= >>=
       &= ^= |=

 15    ,                          Comma operator                 Left-to-right

===============================================================================
                           KEY PRECEDENCE RULES
===============================================================================
1. Primary operators () [] . -> have the highest priority.
2. Unary operators (! ~ ++ -- * & sizeof) evaluate Right-to-Left.
3. Arithmetic (* / %) > (+ -) > Bitwise Shifts (<< >>) > Relational (< >).
4. Relational (< <= > >=) has higher precedence than Equality (== !=).
5. Bitwise (& ^ |) comes BEFORE Logical (&& ||).
6. Assignment operators (= += etc.) have very low precedence and evaluate
   Right-to-Left.
7. Comma operator (,) has the absolute lowest precedence.
===============================================================================
